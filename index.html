Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to travel to the U.S.?
No. Everything is handled 100% remotely. You submit your information online and we handle all filings digitally. Documents are delivered via email.
What is an EIN and why do I need it?
EIN (Employer Identification Number) is your U.S. federal tax ID — like a national ID for your business. You need it to open a bank account and sell on Amazon.
How long does LLC formation take?
Typically 5–7 business days for standard processing. Expedited options available depending on the state. Wyoming and Delaware are fastest.
What is a Fintech bank account?
A Fintech account is a digital U.S. business bank account (e.g. Mercury, Relay) that works like a traditional bank but is accessible fully online — perfect for non-residents.
What is ITIN and who needs it?
ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) is needed for non-U.S. residents to file personal taxes. It's different from EIN — EIN is for your business, ITIN is for you personally.
Why do I need a verified U.S. address?
A unique verified address is required by banks and Amazon for account verification. A generic address (like a PO box) is often rejected. Our unique addresses pass bank checks.
